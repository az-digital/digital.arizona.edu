Latest Versions

Explore the Arizona Digital toolkit for building and maintaining UArizona digital experiences.

Quickstart 3

Quickstart 3 (AZ Quickstart) is the University’s current Drupal 11 distribution. It combines Arizona Bootstrap 5 with best practices for building accessible, scalable sites. Includes features like Paragraphs, Layout Builder, and configuration defaults for UA-branded content.

Get started

Arizona Bootstrap 5

Arizona Bootstrap 5 is a University of Arizona branded version of Bootstrap 5. This release offers new features, improved grid and utility systems, and better accessibility support. It is stable and production-ready, and is integrated into the Quickstart 3 theme.

Explore

Quickstart 2 LTS

Drupal 10 Long Term Support

Quickstart 2 LTS is the University’s long term supported distribution that is pinned to Drupal 10.

End of Life Warning

Quickstart 2 LTS will continue to receive support until December 10, 2026, when Drupal 10 has reached its end of life. Arizona Digital recommends updating all sites to Quickstart 3 as soon as possible. For assistance, reach out on the Support channel on Teams.

Legacy Versions

Arizona Bootstrap 2

Bootstrap 4 Legacy

End of Life Warning

Arizona Bootstrap 2 will continue to receive support until December 10, 2026. Arizona Digital recommends updating all sites and applications to Arizona Bootstrap 5 as soon as possible. See the backwards compatibility page to get started. For assistance, reach out on the Support channel on Teams.

Arizona Bootstrap 2 was the University of Arizona’s version of Bootstrap 4, integrated into the Quickstart 2.0 theme.
